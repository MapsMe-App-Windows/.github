*Maps.me is an offline maps and navigation app for Windows that helps people view detailed maps and get walking, driving, and cycling directions without needing a live internet connection.*

Maps.me for Windows takes its map data from OpenStreetMap and lets you download whole regions in advance, so the app keeps working in places where mobile data is slow, expensive, or simply absent. Many people who search for maps me are looking for exactly this: a map and navigation tool that doesn't stop working the moment the signal drops.

## Why people use it
- `[Offline]` Download entire regions ahead of time so the map and routing keep working with no connection
- `[Navigation]` Get turn-by-turn directions for walking, driving, and cycling
- `[Search]` Look up nearby restaurants, hotels, fuel stations, and other points of interest
- `[Bookmarks]` Save favorite places and organize them for later trips
